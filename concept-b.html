05 — Citizen science

Every spot pattern is a name

Every whale shark carries a unique pattern of spots behind her gills. Photographed from the right angle, it may identify that individual again. A Galápagos Whale Shark Project scientist guides the process aboard; submitted images are reviewed before they can become an accepted contribution.

  1. 01ObserveLearn what makes an identification photograph useful.
  2. 02PhotographRecord the spot pattern without disturbing the animal.
  3. 03SubmitSuitable images may be sent for review.
  4. 04ReviewOnly accepted contributions enter the research record.

Support since 2019 covers satellite tags and tracking time, contributions to Sharkbook.ai, field logistics and data analysis. Satellite tagging and in-water behavioural research stay scientist-led.
