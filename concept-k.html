On to the research

What a guest leaves behind

01 / Partnership

GSD has supported the Galápagos Whale Shark Project since 2019 — satellite tags and tracking time, contributions to Sharkbook.ai, field logistics, and data analysis.

02 / Method

Three methods, no more: photo-identification, satellite tagging, and in-water behavioural research. A spot pattern behind the gills is matched the way star fields are matched.

03 / Your part

One GWSP shark scientist is aboard every Citizen Science departure. Guests contribute photo-ID submissions and standardised observation counts — SharkCount — that go to Sharkbook.ai.

04 / Stakes

Whale sharks are Endangered on the IUCN Red List. Dr Simon J. Pierce led the 2016 global assessment that established that, and the 2017 push that uplisted the species under the UN Convention on Migratory Species.
