Galapagos Shark Diving — homepage design concepts

Fifteen independent homepage design directions, A through O. Each is a real, scrollable, interactive prototype built from GSD's actual content, pricing and photography — not a screenshot or a mockup. Open each one, scroll through it on your own screen (desktop and mobile), and judge the overall design direction rather than small prototype details. This list is unranked and in alphabetical order only — no concept is recommended, and none has been chosen.

Which concepts make Galapagos Shark Diving feel most professional, trustworthy, distinctive and exciting? Note your favorite, a top 3, or any comments and pass them along directly — there's no separate submission system for this round.
