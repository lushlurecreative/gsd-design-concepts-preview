Aboard

Sixteen guests, two vessels, one table.

A liveaboard week is somebody's home for seven days. Cabins are double or twin, meals are all aboard, and the researcher you dive with sits down to dinner with you.

The research you join

Your photograph may be the one that names her.

Every whale shark carries a unique pattern of spots behind the gills. Photographed from the right angle, it identifies that individual anywhere in the world. On Citizen Science departures a GWSP shark scientist teaches you how, in the water, and reviews what you bring back.

Partnership
Galápagos Whale Shark Project, supported every season since 2019
What you do
Photo identification and standardised SharkCount observations
Where it goes
Sharkbook.ai, the international whale shark photo-ID database
