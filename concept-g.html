MODULE 06SCIENCE PAYLOAD · GALÁPAGOS WHALE SHARK PROJECTPARTNERED SINCE 2019
P-01

Scientist aboard

One Galápagos Whale Shark Project scientist sails on every Citizen Science departure. Continuous since 2019.

P-02

Photo identification

Gill-region spot patterns for whale sharks, facial patterns for turtles, ventral patterns for mantas. Submissions feed Sharkbook.ai.

P-03

SharkCount protocol

Standardised observation counts logged across 20 archipelago dive sites, alongside Dive Against Debris survey data.

P-04

Tagging and ultrasound

Satellite tagging, tracking time and field logistics. Ultrasound work added in 2025 to examine whether adult females are pregnant.
